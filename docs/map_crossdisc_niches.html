Case study 3: Mapping and dominating cross-disciplinary niches

Status: draft — persona decided, other items still open

Persona

Individual-researcher framing: “where’s an under-served niche I could credibly move into?” — not the consortium/program-leadership framing (“where are our structural gaps as an organization”). Chosen to match the audience for the grad/postdoc workshop feedback sessions (see Workshop session 2) and to stay consistent with case studies 1 and 2’s persona framing.

Persona — niche-seeking grad student/postdoc. A grad student or postdoc nearing research independence, setting up their first independent research direction (e.g., planning a first R01/K-award). Wants to find an under-served niche adjacent to their current expertise rather than crowd into an already-saturated area, and wants to know which Bridge2AI members are close enough (bridgeable) to be early collaborators in that niche.

Scenario / trigger

Informs an individual’s next research direction, not a consortium hiring/recruitment decision — the junior researcher uses the talent graph to identify a defensible niche and appropriate early collaborators while setting up their independent program.
